# AfterhoursNex for Mac 1.2.S

*How many times do I have left to see you again in my life?*

The first native Mac release of AfterhoursNex: written in Swift for the Mac, in Liquid Glass, with every feature of AfterhoursNex 1.2.

## Made for the Mac

- **The layout and motion of AfterhoursNex 1.2.0.**
  - **The player bar:** a floating glass bar at the bottom of the window, with a slim scrubber you can drag, speed, captions and sleep.
  - **Now Playing:** over the whole window, with the cover blurred behind it, large artwork that eases back when you pause, and the captions rolling beside it. Back, Translate and Captions sit at the top.
  - **The sidebar:** the Auto captions switch at the bottom.
  - **Motion:** pages rise into place on 1.2.0's Fluent curve, and shows and previews zoom in gently. Buttons and cards press and lift.
- **Set like Apple Music.**
  - **Pages:** large titles over each page, and shelves that snap as they scroll.
  - **Artwork:** it lifts under the pointer and shows a play button.
  - **Show pages:** album-style headers tinted with the cover's own colour.
  - **Episodes:** rows highlight under the pointer and while playing, a double-click plays, and play buttons wear a ring for how far through you are.
- **Search as you type.** Results appear while you type, on a page of their own, with a clear message when nothing matches or something goes wrong.
- **Liquid Glass, as macOS 27 refines it.**
  - **A floating glass sidebar,** and the player in a glass toolbar.
  - **Now Playing's controls:** round glass buttons that come together into one surface.
  - **Hero art:** a show's artwork extends softly under the sidebar.
  - **Readability first:** text never sits on glass. Choose Regular or Clear glass, and everything turns solid when Reduce Transparency is on.
- **The Mac's own controls.**
  - **The system:** Now Playing in the menu bar and Control Center, and your keyboard's media keys.
  - **Menus and shortcuts:** a Controls menu with standard shortcuts, ⌘[ to go back, File › Import and Export OPML, and Edit › Undo for a show you removed.
  - **Settings:** a standard Settings window, opened with ⌘,.
- **Apple's own intelligence, on your Mac.**
  - **Captions:** made with your choice of speech model in Settings › Captions. SpeechAnalyzer is Apple's newer model for long recordings, with language models macOS downloads once. Classic speech recognition knows the most languages. Automatic picks between them for each language.
  - **Translation:** captions translated with Apple's translation, and the languages managed by macOS.
- **Fast and light.**
  - **Covers:** decoded at the size they're shown, and held in a memory cache with a firm limit.
  - **Feeds:** read away from the interface, so scrolling stays smooth.
  - **Lists:** they redraw only when something they show changes.
  - **The app itself:** there's no runtime to bundle, so it's small and quick to open.

## Everything from AfterhoursNex 1.2

- **The starter shelf:** thirty hand-picked shows the first time you open the app, and the full catalogue in Discover.
- **Search:** for shows, or for single episodes on any topic, with previews of shows and episodes before you add them.
- **Radio:** public FM and AM stations by region.
- **Video podcasts:** YouTube and Bilibili, as audio. One click in Settings › Video adds the video tool, about 35 MB.
- **Rolling captions:** from transcripts, caption files or your Mac, with translations underneath.
- **Your library:**
  - **Arranging:** rename, pin, group and drag shows into order, and change a show's feed address.
  - **Covers:** choose an image or design one.
  - **Your own audio:** import audio files, or drop them on the window.
- **On your phone:**
  - **In a browser:** phones on the same Wi-Fi can use AfterhoursNex through a private link and a QR code.
  - **The Android app:** it can use this Mac's captions and video podcasts.
- **In English, 简体中文 and 日本語.**
- **Update checks:** AfterhoursNex tells you when a new version is out.

## Download

`AfterhoursNex-Mac-1.2.S.dmg`: a universal app, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 26 or later.

## Good to know

- **The first launch:** unless the app is signed with a Developer ID, macOS asks before opening it. Right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- **Phone access:** macOS asks whether AfterhoursNex can use your local network.
- **Auto captions:** macOS asks before AfterhoursNex uses speech recognition.
- **Phones in a browser:** captions made on the Mac go to the AfterhoursNex Android app, not yet to the phone page in a browser.
